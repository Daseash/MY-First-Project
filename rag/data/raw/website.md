# How WanderLust Works — Site Help

## What is WanderLust?
WanderLust is a travel & homestay booking platform. It lets travelers browse and book unique stays — beachfront cottages, mountain cabins, luxury villas, treehouses, castles and more — from hosts around the world. Prices are shown in Indian Rupees (₹).

## Creating an Account
- Go to the **Sign Up** page and enter a username, email and password.
- After signing up you are logged in automatically.
- Existing users can **Log In** with their username and password.
- Use **Log Out** to end your session. Authentication is handled with sessions and cookies.

## Browsing & Searching Stays
- The **home page** shows a handful of featured listings.
- The **listings page** shows every stay. Use the **search box** to filter by title, description, location or country (e.g. type "villa" or "Goa").
- Click any listing card to open its detail page with photos, description, price, location and guest reviews.

## The Booking Widget
- On a listing's detail page you can pick a **check-in date** and **check-out date**.
- Choose the **number of guests** (1–16) with the +/- controls.
- The total updates live: it multiplies the nightly price by the number of nights, then adds a **cleaning fee (8%)** and a **service fee (12%)**.
- Once dates are selected the **Reserve button** shows your total (e.g. "Reserve — ₹4,860"). Minimum check-in is today; check-out must be after check-in.

## Reviews
- Logged-in guests can leave a **review** on a listing with a **1–5 star rating** and a comment.
- The listing page shows the average rating and all reviews.
- You can delete your own review at any time.

## Hosting Your Own Place
- Hosts can **Add a new listing** via the "New Listing" form (title, description, price, location, country, image).
- You can **Edit** or **Delete** your listings later. Deleting a listing also removes all of its reviews.

## Payments & Pricing
- All prices are per night in **Indian Rupees (₹)**.
- The final price breakdown is: nights × price + 8% cleaning fee + 12% service fee.
- Refunds follow the cancellation policy (see the Cancellation Policy section).

## The AI Concierge (E.D.I.T.H.)
- The floating **EDITH** button opens a chat assistant.
- You can ask it to **find stays** ("villa with a pool in Phuket", "stays under ₹1500"), ask about **policies** ("what's your pet policy?"), or ask **how the site works** ("how do I create an account?").
- EDITH answers using the real listings and policies on the site.

## Need More Help?
- Check the **Cancellation Policy**, **Booking Policy**, **Pet Policy** and **House Rules** pages.
- For anything else, ask EDITH directly or use the site's contact options.
