# WanderLust Policies & FAQs

## Cancellation Policy
- **Free cancellation** is available for all stays up to **48 hours** before check-in.
- Cancellations between 48 hours and 24 hours before check-in are refunded at **50%**.
- Cancellations within **24 hours** of check-in are non-refundable.
- No-shows are charged the full booking amount and are non-refundable.

## Booking Policy
- A valid payment method is required to confirm any reservation.
- Check-in time is from **2:00 PM** local time; check-out is by **11:00 AM**.
- Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability and an additional fee.

## Pet Policy
- **Pet-friendly** stays allow dogs and cats up to 2 pets per booking.
- Pet stays may carry a small cleaning fee which is shown before you confirm.
- Pets must not be left unattended in the listing.

## House Rules
- Please respect quiet hours between **10:00 PM and 8:00 AM**.
- Smoking is only permitted in designated outdoor areas.
- Treat every home with care — report any accidental damage to the host immediately.

## Frequently Asked Questions
- **Can I change my booking dates?** Yes, date changes are free more than 48 hours before check-in, subject to availability.
- **Is breakfast included?** Breakfast is included only in listings that explicitly mention it in their description.
- **How do refunds work?** Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.
